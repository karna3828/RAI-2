About this demo

All three companies are fictional. Every number comes from the sample dataset in this file (const D), in ₹ crore, for an illustrative FY 2025-26 (Apr–Mar).

The "AI" is a rule-based prototype: fixed rules over sample data, no external model. Forecasts are straight-line trends, not predictions. Unusual patterns are signals to investigate, not proof of misconduct.
